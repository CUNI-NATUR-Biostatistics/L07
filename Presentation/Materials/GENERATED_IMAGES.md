## Title generation, 2026-10-11

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool using five inspected local reference assets: the two photographs and detective listed above, `spolecna_zahrada_s_kufry.png` (existing AI-generated series illustration), and the embedded PNG from `Presentation/presentation.html`, slide „Aditivní model: rovnoběžné přímky“ (lesson-authored figure; extracted temporarily for generation). The photograph reuse terms remain CC0 for Jasper Shide and CC BY 4.0 for Cao et al. (2025); the existing title caption retains the latter attribution. The scatter plot informed only the parallel-path metaphor and categorical palette; no data values, labels, or charts were reproduced.

The planned prompt above was normalized for reference roles and the existing purple title background. Final output: 1672 × 941 pixels (approximately 16:9), RGBA, alpha range 0–255, 53.77% fully transparent pixels. The built-in output was copied without changing its pixels.
